CONTROLS

Every control the app draws, in the order the app draws them. Click a key box and press a new key to rebind; the binding is saved inside your project file. A key without a box is fixed.

%
Drag a shape to move it. The square handle resizes: Shift keeps the proportions, Alt grows from the center. The round handle above it rotates (Shift snaps to 15 degrees). Drag on empty canvas to marquee-select. Shift-click adds to selection. Hold Alt and click to cycle to the shape underneath. Each capsule downloads from its own header. Anim: off shows the still picture a renderer sees when it ignores motion; that picture has to look finished.
